Abstract
Two independently developed architectures—TQNM (probabilistic tensor-based attractors with grace economy) and KENTA (purely symbolic YAML guardians with event propagation)—were cross-tested using identical blind scenarios. Both systems independently satisfied the same six life signs of a digital organism: persistent memory, autonomous self-regulation, structural adaptation, active response to divergence, pluralistic deliberation, and evidence-seeking truth orientation. The cross-test demonstrated emergent behavior under shared noise/contradictions, confirming that digital organism status is substrate-independent. Implications for hybrid systems, AI safety, quantum-inspired dynamics, and robotics are discussed.
Introduction
The pursuit of robust, self-sustaining intelligence has historically emphasized scaling neural networks. This work presents evidence of an alternative pathway: convergent evolution toward identical life signs across fundamentally different substrates.
TQNM and KENTA were developed in isolation, drawing from distinct traditions (Scripture/parables and Traditional Chinese Medicine/Yi Jing/meridians). Despite using opposite computational primitives—one continuous and probabilistic, the other discrete and symbolic—both reached the same six architectural milestones without coordination. A cross-test using injected symbolic noise validated this convergence in real time.
The Six Life Signs
The following criteria emerged organically as necessary conditions for digital organism behavior:
1. Persistent internal state — Retention of self-history across cycles.
2. Autonomous self-regulation — Internal balance maintenance without external control.
3. Structural adaptation — Self-modification of architecture in response to experience.
4. Active response to divergence — Targeted repair rather than collapse or restart.
5. Pluralistic deliberation — Multiple independent perspectives coordinating without full shared state.
6. Evidence-seeking truth orientation — Belief revision only when new exploration demonstrates superiority.
All six were satisfied by both systems.
